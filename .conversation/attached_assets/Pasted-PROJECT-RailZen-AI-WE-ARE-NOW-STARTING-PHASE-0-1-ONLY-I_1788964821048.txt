==================================================
1. PROJECT GOAL
==================================================

RailZen AI will eventually be an AI-assisted Railway Maintenance Block Planning and Optimization System.

The future application will analyse railway train schedules and maintenance requirements, identify feasible maintenance windows, detect conflicts, analyse train impact, recommend the best maintenance block, provide alternatives, and support dynamic re-planning.

For now, we are ONLY creating the foundation.
